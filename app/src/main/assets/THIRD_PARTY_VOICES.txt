British male kill-streak recordings
==================================

The kill_streak_*.wav resources are generated speech for SimpleCoil's existing
English kill-streak prompts. The voice is bm_george (British male) from
Kokoro v1.0, generated offline with kokoro-onnx 0.6.1.
